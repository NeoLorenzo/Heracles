How it works

Follow one set all the way through.

Train, record, interpret, track. Heracles keeps what you did separate from what it infers, so every number further down the chain can be traced back to the set that produced it.

01

Train

Start from a preset or from scratch. Heracles lays out your exercises with blank sets and puts last session's performance on the same exercise and equipment right next to them.

Entered · your workout

02

Record

Log load, reps and reps in reserve. Every set is classified the moment it is complete, and the edit is saved in the background — even when the gym has no signal.

Entered · load, reps, RIR

03

Interpret

Two equations, run on completed reps and on reps plus reported RIR, give four strength estimates. They are kept apart — never averaged into one false certainty. The exercise's authored relevance row turns the set into weighted sets per muscle.

Calculated · estimate  ·  Authored · model

04

Track

One representative working set per session and equipment becomes a point in your progression, drawn as the full range of its four estimates. Body weight, tape measurements and muscle exposure sit alongside as separate evidence.

Product principles

What Heracles refuses to calculate matters too.

Many apps multiply weight × reps and call it volume. Twenty-five easy reps with a light weight can out-score eight hard ones with a heavy weight, and a leg press out-scores a leg extension by sheer stack size. Tonnage measures mass moved — not effort, mechanics or which muscles did the work — so Heracles never uses it as a training, progress or hypertrophy metric.

Preserve the observation. Label the interpretation. Expose the assumptions. Do not manufacture certainty where the evidence does not support it.
Why tonnage is excluded →
↗

Performance stays exercise-specific

Load and repetitions are interpreted within the same movement and equipment context instead of being compared across mechanically different exercises.

≠

Tonnage is not called volume

Weight × repetitions measures external mass moved repeatedly. It is not treated as hypertrophic stimulus, workload quality or progression.

◎

Estimates stay plural

Observed and RIR-adjusted strength estimates remain separate, making disagreement between models visible instead of hiding it behind an average.

§

Uncertainty remains visible

Recorded observations, calculated estimates, authored models, scientific evidence and product decisions are presented as different kinds of knowledge.

Longitudinal evidence

Different signals stay different.

The system does not force training performance, body weight, circumference and modelled muscle relevance into a universal progress score. Each answers a different question.

          Methodology →

          Each working set (reported RIR 0–3) contributes between 0 and 1 weighted set to a muscle or group based on the exercise's authored hypertrophic-relevance mapping. Warm-ups and 4+ RIR sets are excluded by this product rule; that does not mean 4+ RIR produces literally zero hypertrophy. This estimates worthwhile muscular involvement, not growth, force, or “effective sets.”

          Over time

          What trained it

          Exercise progression

          Each qualifying set preserves Brzycki and Epley estimates from completed reps plus both estimates using reps + reported RIR. This four-value model spread is not a confidence interval or a measured max. 4+ RIR sets are excluded. Relative mode divides each value independently by body weight on its workout date.

          Last 4 weeks vs previous 4 weeks

          Recent change

          Percentage change uses the previous 4 weeks as the baseline. “New this period” is shown when that baseline is zero. Increases are green and decreases are red, with a signed text label so color is never the only cue. Changes are descriptive and are not recommendations about how much a muscle should be trained.

          How these numbers are calculated

          Sessions counts dated workout records in the selected period. Working sets includes non-warm-up sets with reported RIR 0–3; warm-ups and 4+ RIR sets remain in history but are excluded. Exercises trained counts exact catalogue exercise names with a working set.

          Modelled muscle exposure adds the authored exercise–muscle relevance coefficient for every working set. A UI group's value uses the highest child-muscle coefficient for each set, preventing groups with more anatomical entities from being inflated.

          Exercise progression selects one representative RIR 0–3 working set per equipment identity and session using completed performance only: higher lower observed Brzycki/Epley estimate, then higher upper estimate, load, and reps. It then plots four inspectable values: observed Brzycki and Epley, and both formulas using completed reps + reported RIR.

          Relative estimated 1RM divides all four calculated values independently by measured or interpolated body weight on the workout date. It is shown in × BW, is unavailable outside body-weight coverage, and never changes stored absolute estimates.

                Scientific foundations

                Literature

                A transparent record of the evidence, authored models, assumptions, and limits behind the app.

                Our standard

                Useful claims require more than a citation

                The app separates published evidence, calculated estimates, authored product models, and product decisions. A value is not presented as scientific fact merely because it is numerical.

                Evaluation protocol 01

                How studies are judged

                Outcome evidence is assessed claim by claim. The protocol prioritizes direct measurement, trained populations, appropriate comparators, adherence, uncertainty, and whether the study actually isolates the variable being discussed.

                • Evidence is weighted rather than counted as votes.
                • Non-significance is not treated as proof of equivalence.
                • Study quality is not collapsed into a magic numerical score.
                Authored taxonomy 02

                Muscle-group taxonomy

                The hypertrophy model begins with 40 muscle entities chosen at the highest anatomical resolution that is both useful and defensible. Muscles are separated when joint function, joint-crossing anatomy, longitudinal evidence, or exercise modelling makes the distinction meaningful.

                • Distinct hypertrophic responses justify added granularity.
                • Regional growth alone does not automatically create a new muscle entity.
                • The taxonomy remains revisable as stronger evidence emerges.
                Authored model 03

                Movement-pattern coefficients

                The movement matrix organizes exercises across biomechanical dimensions. Its 0–1 coefficients are expert-authored contribution ratings—not percentages, probabilities, measured joint torques, or direct estimates of muscular stimulus.

                • Each movement pattern is an independent dimension.
                • Row totals have no scientific meaning.
                • Small coefficient differences must not be presented as exact.
                Functional-anatomy model 04

                Movement pattern to muscle function

                This 40 × 40 matrix records which canonical muscles are anatomically capable of materially producing each movement pattern. Its coefficients are functional priors, not force shares or estimates of hypertrophic stimulus.

                • Coefficients are independent and must not be row-normalized.
                • Joint angle and exercise context can change force-sharing between synergists.
                • Exercise-specific modelling remains a separate downstream layer.
                Derived functional model 05

                Exercise to muscle composition

                The exercise × muscle matrix is the transparent product of the exercise-to-pattern and pattern-to-muscle matrices. It traces every exercise to every canonical muscle without pretending the result is a measured hypertrophy score.

                • Each cell is a sum of movement-path products.
                • Raw scores are never normalized or capped and may exceed one.
                • The model preserves both upstream version identities.
                Exercise-specific model 06

                Hypertrophic relevance by exercise

                This authored 116 × 40 layer filters anatomical possibility through exercise context. It assigns worthwhile hypertrophic relevance under the assumption of one sufficiently hard set, intended technique, and a normal/full useful range of motion.

                • Values range from 0 to 1 in five documented relevance levels.
                • Coefficients are independent, not percentages, and are never normalized.
                • Effort, set count, progression, execution, and fatigue remain separate downstream variables.
                Model limitations 07

                Current mapping limitations

                The present exercise → movement pattern → muscle function pipeline is a useful first-pass anatomical model, but it is not yet calibrated as a quantitative model of hypertrophic stimulus.

                • Joint actions do not determine force-sharing between synergists.
                • Muscle length, resistance profiles, and biarticular interference remain incomplete.
                • Longitudinal exercise-specific hypertrophy evidence is not yet a calibration layer.
                Product decision 08

                Why tonnage is excluded

                External mass multiplied by repetitions is not used as a hypertrophy, workload, progression, or personal-statistics metric. It ignores effort, biomechanics, resistance profiles, technique, muscle contribution, and differences between exercises.

                • Load and repetitions remain useful as separate performance data.
                • Hard working sets are a more relevant starting point for training exposure.
                • Decorative metrics are omitted when they cannot guide a decision.
                Product standard 09

                Design rules

                The app's product standard requires transparent calculations, defensible metrics, owner-scoped data, and interfaces that expose the meaning and limitations of modelled results.

                • No magic math or unexplained scores.
                • Scientific uncertainty must remain visible.
                • Fewer meaningful metrics are preferred to decorative statistics.
                Calculated estimate 10

                Estimated one-rep max

                The app reports a range from the Brzycki and Epley equations using the logged load and repetitions. This is an estimate of exercise-specific performance, not a direct measurement of maximal strength.

                Brzycki weight × 36 ÷ (37 − reps)

                Epley weight × (1 + reps ÷ 30)

                The range is omitted when the inputs are incomplete or outside Brzycki’s 1–36 repetition domain. Equipment, technique, fatigue, and individual response can all affect its accuracy.

                !

                Evidence library status

                The study-selection protocol is a working specification and still contains unresolved citation placeholders. Those passages are not treated as fully sourced claims. Exact study records and bibliographic references will be added here as the evidence library is reviewed.
